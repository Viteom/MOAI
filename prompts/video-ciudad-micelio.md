# Prompt de video · Ciudad Micelio (One Health)

Versión 16:9 (para web) y versión 9:16 (para el teléfono). El logo y el texto se agregan después en edición, no en el video.

```
Cinematic aerial-to-street journey through "Micelio", a dreamed solarpunk city built on the One Health principle: the health of people, animals and nature is one single system. Photorealistic, warm golden-hour light, ultra detailed, 16:9.

World: terraced hillside city inside a green valley, giant transparent geodesic domes covered in climbing vines, elevated wooden walkways, waterfalls feeding clear canals and wetland gardens that filter the water, living-wall towers, rooftop orchards, a distant mountain range under a deep blue sky with soft clouds. Glowing mycelium threads run under glass-floor sections of the paths, pulsing softly in lime green and cyan, connecting every house, tree and garden like a living network.

One Health in every frame: elderly people and children walking together; a grandmother feeding a stray dog; a vet clinic open to the garden; bees and hummingbirds pollinating along the walkways; a cat asleep on a window ledge; neighbors planting a community garden; birds nesting inside the domes; people, animals and plants sharing the same spaces, no cars.

Camera and beats (about 12 s):
0-4 s slow drone descent from the canopy, framed by large sunlit leaves in the foreground, revealing the domes and waterfall.
4-8 s glide along a walkway behind two neighbors and a dog, passing a glass floor where the mycelium lights up as they pass.
8-12 s sun sets, the city turns bioluminescent: lime and cyan neon filaments glow through leaves, roots and rooftops, a cyberpunk-botanical night, then settle on a wide shot with empty space in the upper center for a title.

Style: solarpunk meets bioluminescent cyber-botanical concept art, saturated greens, deep cobalt sky, cream highlights, neon lime accents. Smooth continuous camera motion, shallow depth of field in the foreground leaves, gentle ambient motion (water, leaves, birds).

Negative: no text, no logos, no watermark, no cars, no guns, no dystopian grime, no distorted faces or hands, no flickering, no morphing.
```
